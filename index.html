What Are Duplex Blinds? A Quick Overview

Duplex blinds are a modern, double-layered window covering that combines alternating bands of sheer (translucent) and opaque fabric on a single roller mechanism. As the two layers slide past each other, you can align the sheer sections for soft, diffused daylight, or overlap the opaque bands for complete privacy and room darkening — or stop anywhere in between.

They are also called:

  • Zebra blinds — because of the striped alternating fabric pattern
  • Day and night blinds — reflecting their dual-function light control
  • Vision blinds — used in the UK and Gulf market
  • Combi blinds or double roller blinds — common in commercial settings
  • Dimout blinds — when partial light reduction is the primary goal

Unlike standard roller blinds or venetian blinds, duplex blinds give you a continuous spectrum of light control without sacrificing style. They look sleek, sit flat against the window, and complement virtually every interior — from minimalist apartments in Dubai Marina to corporate offices in Business Bay.
